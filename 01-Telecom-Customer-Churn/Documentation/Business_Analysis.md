# Business Analysis

## Dataset
- **Dataset:** Telco Customer Churn
- **Total Customers:** 7,043
- **Churned Customers:** 1,869
- **Active Customers:** 5,174
- **Overall Churn Rate:** 26.54%
- **Retention Rate:** 73.46%

---

# Business Question 1

## What is the overall customer churn rate?

### Business Insight

Out of **7,043 customers**, **1,869 customers left the company**, resulting in an overall **churn rate of 26.54%**.

Approximately **1 in every 4 customers** has discontinued the service.

### Recommendation

- Improve customer retention programs.
- Identify high-risk customers early.
- Focus on reducing monthly customer cancellations.

---

# Business Question 2

## Which contract type has the highest churn?

| Contract | Total Customers | Churned Customers | Churn Rate |
|----------|---------------:|------------------:|-----------:|
| Month-to-month | 3875 | 1655 | **42.71%** |
| One year | 1473 | 166 | 11.27% |
| Two year | 1695 | 48 | 2.83% |

### Business Insight

Customers with **Month-to-Month contracts** have an extremely high churn rate compared to long-term contract customers.

### Recommendation

- Encourage customers to switch to annual plans.
- Offer discounts for yearly subscriptions.
- Launch renewal campaigns for month-to-month customers.

# Business Question 3

## Which payment method has the highest churn?

| Payment Method | Churn Rate |
|---------------|-----------:|
| Electronic Check | **45.29%** |
| Mailed Check | 19.11% |
| Bank Transfer (Automatic) | 16.71% |
| Credit Card (Automatic) | 15.24% |

### Business Insight

Customers paying via **Electronic Check** churn almost three times more than customers using automatic payment methods.

- Offer cashback or discounts for automatic payments.
- Improve the electronic payment experience.

# Key Findings

- Overall churn rate is **26.54%**.
- **Month-to-Month contracts** have the highest churn (42.71%).
- **Electronic Check** customers churn the most (45.29%).
- **Fiber Optic** users have the highest churn among internet services (41.89%).
- **Senior Citizens** churn significantly more (41.68%).
- Customers **without Partners** and **without Dependents** are more likely to churn.
- Customers without **Tech Support**, **Online Security**, or **Device Protection** have much higher churn rates.
- Entertainment services like **Streaming TV** and **Streaming Movies** have only a modest effect on customer retention.

# Final Business Recommendations

1. Convert Month-to-Month customers into annual contracts.
2. Promote automatic payment methods.
3. Improve Fiber Optic service quality and customer experience.
4. Bundle Tech Support, Online Security, and Device Protection with internet plans.
5. Develop targeted retention programs for Senior Citizens.
6. Create family and partner bundle offers.
7. Improve the Paperless Billing experience.
8. Use entertainment services as value-added features rather than primary retention tools.
