# 📊 Operations Performance Dashboard | Power BI

A comprehensive multi-page **Power BI dashboard** built to monitor operational performance, employee productivity, customer satisfaction, attendance, and process-level analytics.

This project demonstrates the use of **Power BI**, **DAX**, **data modeling**, and **interactive dashboards** to transform operational data into actionable business insights.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

The Operations Performance Dashboard provides management with a centralized view of key operational metrics through interactive visualizations and slicers.

The dashboard enables users to:

- Monitor overall business performance
- Analyze operational trends over time
- Identify top and bottom performing employees
- Measure customer satisfaction
- Compare process and team performance
- Track attendance and productivity metrics

---

# 📂 Dashboard Pages

## 1️⃣ Executive Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of organizational performance.

### KPIs

- Total Employees
- Total Cases Completed
- Total Login Hours
- Total Productive Hours
- Total Overtime Hours
- Productivity %
- Average Quality Score
- Average CSAT
- Average Adherence
- Average Handle Time

### Filters

- Year
- Month
- Country
- Process
- Team
- Manager

---

## 2️⃣ Operations Trends

Tracks monthly operational performance.

### Visualizations

- Monthly Cases Completed Trend
- Monthly Login Hours Trend
- Monthly Productive vs Idle Hours

Purpose:
- Identify monthly performance patterns
- Detect seasonal trends
- Monitor operational efficiency

---

## 3️⃣ Employee Performance

Analyzes employee productivity.

### Visualizations

- Top 10 Employees by Cases Completed
- Bottom 10 Employees by Cases Completed
- Top 10 Employees by Productivity %
- Bottom 10 Employees by Productivity %

Purpose:
- Identify high performers
- Detect employees requiring improvement
- Support performance management

---

## 4️⃣ Employee Satisfaction

Measures employee quality and customer satisfaction.

### Visualizations

- Top 10 Employees by CSAT
- Bottom 10 Employees by CSAT
- Top 10 Employees by Quality Score
- Bottom 10 Employees by Quality Score

Purpose:
- Monitor customer satisfaction
- Evaluate employee quality performance

---

## 5️⃣ Process & Team Analysis

Compares operational performance across business units.

### Visualizations

- Top Processes by Cases Completed
- Top Teams by Cases Completed
- Top Managers by Cases Completed
- Cases Completed by Country

Purpose:
- Compare business processes
- Evaluate manager performance
- Analyze regional performance

---

## 6️⃣ Attendance & Productivity

Provides workforce utilization insights.

### Visualizations

- Employees by Attendance Status
- Average Adherence by Team
- Productivity % by Process
- Average Handle Time by Shift
- Productivity % by Team

Purpose:
- Analyze attendance trends
- Monitor productivity
- Evaluate operational efficiency
